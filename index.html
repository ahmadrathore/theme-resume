About

I am Technical Program Director at Pakistan’s National CERT (NCERT), leading the Technical Development Center on vulnerability assessment, penetration testing, and coordinated disclosure across federal, provincial, and private-sector institutions. I previously led Cloud & HPC operations at the University of New Hampshire (2,200+ CPU cores, hybrid AWS migrations, CrowdStrike/Palo Alto hardening) and production AWS platforms at Northwestern University serving 15,000+ monthly users.

My research and engineering work spans cloud-native edge systems, microservice orchestration, digital pathology tooling, and federated research networks (OF@TEIN / Asi@Connect). I publish in Q1 venues and teach networking, distributed computing, and Python automation.
