About

I am Technical Program Director at National CERT – Pakistan, leading national-scale cybersecurity programs, AI-enabled vulnerability management, VDP, and proactive cyber defense. I previously served as Assistant Professor at NUST (SEECS), Islamabad. My career spans data center modernization, cloud migrations, and HPC operations across the US, Canada, South Korea, and Pakistan.

Previously Cloud & HPC Operations Manager at the University of New Hampshire and Postdoctoral Fellow at Northwestern University (Feinberg School of Medicine), building GPU-accelerated digital pathology platforms with Kitware. Earlier research includes Asi@Connect / OF@TEIN federated edge systems and ML for cybersecurity at the Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity (UNB).
