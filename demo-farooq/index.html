About

Dr. Farooq Azam Rathore is a Professor and Consultant in Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation (PM&R) at the Combined Military Hospital, Quetta Cantonment, where he serves as Head of Department. He is also Professor at the Quetta Institute of Medical Sciences. His clinical work covers acute and chronic pain (including interventional procedures), musculoskeletal and rheumatological care, spinal cord injury, stroke and neurorehabilitation, multiple sclerosis, and peripheral neuropathies.

He is an approved supervisor and FCPS Part II examiner with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Pakistan (CPSP). Credentials listed on his ORCID profile include FCPS in Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation (2009), MSc in Pain Medicine (2017), a Master’s in Bioethics (2022), and a Certificate in Health Professional Education (2022). He describes himself as Co-Editor in Chief of the Journal of the International Society of Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation.

Internationally, LinkedIn lists him as Secretary of ISPRM (elected May 2026; first person from South Asia in that cabinet role, per his profile) and National Representative of Pakistan to ISPRM (since Feb 2018). ORCID and a Cochrane disclosure form list him as an Advisory Board Member of Cochrane Rehabilitation. He has delivered invited and keynote talks widely; an ISPRM YouTube recording features his ISPRM 2025 Marrakech keynote on AI in rehabilitation medicine (future and ethics).
